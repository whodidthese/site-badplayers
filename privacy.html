Privacy Policy

Badplayers is designed for private, offline listening. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information or usage activity.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. No data collection

Badplayers does not collect, transmit, sell, or use personal information, listening history, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

2. Audio and app data

Audio imported from Files is copied into the app’s local sandbox. Playlists, artwork, settings, and playback position are also stored locally and are not uploaded to a Badplayers server.

3. Photos access

When you choose custom artwork, the system photo picker provides only the image you select. That image is stored locally for use inside the app.

4. This website

This website does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, accounts, or cookies. A language preference may be stored locally in your browser and is not sent to us.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue in the Badplayers support repository. Contact support.
